New peer-reviewed research has found that **ambient AI** and **computer vision** can effectively measure perioperative workflow variation at scale, achieving model accuracy, precision, and recall exceeding 98%. This development has the potential to improve healthcare efficiency and patient outcomes.

Researchers have developed a new **video-based AI tool** for autism assessment, using **computer vision** to track body movement and provide detailed data. This tool is more accessible and cost-effective than traditional methods, making it a promising development for clinicians and families. The use of AI in autism assessment has the potential to improve diagnosis and treatment outcomes, and this new tool is a significant step forward in this area.
