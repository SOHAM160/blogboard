## AI-Generated Stories Rated Higher Than Human-Written Ones
A recent study found that **AI-generated stories** were rated as more absorbing and of higher quality than **human-written stories** by participants. However, when participants were told that a story was written by a human, they gave it higher ratings. This study suggests that **simpler writing** generated by **AI** may be easier to read and digest, but it does not mean that **human authors** are obsolete. The study's findings have significant implications for the **creative writing** industry, as **Generative AI** continues to advance and improve.
