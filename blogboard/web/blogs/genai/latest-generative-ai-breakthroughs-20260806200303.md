# Latest Generative AI News and Breakthroughs
The field of **Generative AI** has witnessed significant advancements in recent times, with researchers and institutions making notable contributions to its development and application. This week, several breakthroughs have been reported, showcasing the potential of **Generative AI** in various domains.

## University of Minnesota Advances AI Breakthroughs with DOE Funding
The University of Minnesota has received funding from the **DOE Genesis Mission** to advance **AI breakthroughs** in areas such as **geothermal energy extraction**, **critical mineral recovery**, and **semiconductor manufacturing**. The research team will utilize **Generative AI** to develop a real-time "**digital twin**" of underground environments, enabling rapid processing of sensor data and providing more accurate insights than current models. This innovation has the potential to revolutionize the energy and manufacturing sectors.

## Leading AI Scholar Elected to Hong Kong Academy of Engineering
**Prof Xie Haoran**, a prominent **AI scholar**, has been elected as a new member of the **Hong Kong Academy of Engineering Young Member Section**. His influential contributions to **Generative AI research** include improving the **training stability** and **image generation quality** of **Generative models**. This recognition highlights the growing importance of **AI research** and its applications in various fields.

## AI-Generated Stories Rated Higher than Human-Written Ones
A recent study has found that **AI-generated stories** are rated as more **absorbing** and of **higher quality** than **human-written stories**. However, participants who were informed that a story was written by a human gave higher ratings than those who were told it was written by **AI**. The study suggests that **simpler writing** by **AI** is easier to read and digest, but this does not imply that human authors are obsolete. This breakthrough has significant implications for the future of **creative writing** and **content generation**.
