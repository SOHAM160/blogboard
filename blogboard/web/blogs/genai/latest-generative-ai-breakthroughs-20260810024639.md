## AI-Generated Stories Outperform Human-Written Ones
A recent study published in the journal **Judgment and Decision Making** found that **AI-generated stories** were rated higher in quality and engagement than human-written ones. The study involved 1,682 adults who read and rated short stories written by humans and **ChatGPT**. The results suggest that **generative AI** has made significant progress in creating compelling and high-quality content, potentially disrupting traditional storytelling and content creation.
