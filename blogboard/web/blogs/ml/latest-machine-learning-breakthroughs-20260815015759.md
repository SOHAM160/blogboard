The use of **AI** in skin disease diagnosis can also help reduce the burden on healthcare systems, allowing for more efficient and effective patient care.

Researchers have made a significant breakthrough in detecting **solar eruptions** using **AI models**. This development has the potential to improve our understanding and prediction of **space weather events**, allowing for better preparation and mitigation of their effects on Earth's magnetic field and satellite systems. The use of **AI** in solar eruption detection can also facilitate a deeper understanding of the Sun's activity and its impact on our planet.
