## Real-Time Experimentation with DONUT
Researchers at the U.S. Department of Energy's (DOE) **Argonne National Laboratory** are utilizing a new machine learning tool called **DONUT** to revolutionize experimentation at the **Advanced Photon Source (APS)**, a DOE Office of Science user facility. This innovative tool delivers results in **real-time**, enabling scientists to make faster decisions and gain deeper insights into the materials they are studying.

## The Singularity: A Point of No Return
The concept of the **singularity**, or the point at which **artificial intelligence** surpasses human intelligence, is becoming increasingly relevant. **Demis Hassabis**, the founder and CEO of **DeepMind**, has stated that we are "standing in the foothills of the singularity." This notion has sparked significant interest, with several top researchers leaving **Google** to launch a new startup called **Discovery Loop**, which aims to accelerate and automate **machine-learning** research and scientific discovery.

## Google's AI Empire Expansion and Brain Drain
**Google** is expanding its **AI** empire, but at the cost of losing the people who built it. The company's chief scientist, **Jeff Dean**, is leaving along with several other top researchers to start **Discovery Loop**, a startup that will focus on automating **machine learning** and scientific discovery. This significant brain drain highlights the challenges faced by tech giants in retaining top talent in the competitive **AI** landscape.
